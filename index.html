About NEXDIGI

Your Global Partner in Career Excellence and Professional Development

Leading the Future of Career Development

NEXDIGI is a forward-thinking placement guidance and career-building organization dedicated to empowering students with industry-relevant skills, professional training, and impactful internship opportunities. We are officially registered under MSME, DPIIT, ISO, and Startup India, ensuring national-level credibility, innovation, and quality across all our training and placement programs.

100+ Industry Partnerships

We partner with leading MNCs including GI Tech, CoderScore, Elite Safety, Ramco Cements, Ramco Systems, Alstom, Sembcorp, ITC, Lifecell, Zoho, and many prestigious companies across IT and core sectors.

Global Reach - 4 Countries
